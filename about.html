About Gloss Nails

Inclusive beauty, exceptional art, and a vibrant community atmosphere.

Gloss Nails proudly opened its doors on Windmill Road, Crumlin in March 2020 as a bright and buzzy neighbourhood joint specialising in bespoke hand-painted nails and trend-led looks. In March 2024, four years after opening the original salon, Gloss Nails Dublin reopened after significant renovation and investment. The salon now boasts a large, experienced team of nail technicians and includes a wide variety of services including luxury pedicures.

As a queer-owned, inclusive nail salon, Gloss Nails champions diversity and celebrates self-expression. They cater to all clients and guarantee that anyone who chooses Gloss Nails for their manicure will be treated with the care and respect they deserve. Owner and creative director Aaron Daly has trained with some of the best names in the industry, creating a welcoming and vibrant atmosphere where every digit becomes a piece of art.
